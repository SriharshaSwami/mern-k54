Battlegrounds Mobile India (abbreviated as BGMI) is an online multiplayer battle royale game developed and published by Krafton. The game is exclusively for the Indian market. It is a version of PUBG Mobile, which was banned in India by the Government of India in September 2020.

The game was released on 2 July 2021 for Android devices, and on 18 August 2021 for iOS devices.

Gameplay

Battlegrounds Mobile India is a player-versus-player shooter game in which up to 100 players fight in a battle royale, a type of large-scale last man standing deathmatch where players fight to remain the last alive. Players can choose to enter the match solo, duo, or in a small team of up to four people.

Each match starts with players parachuting from a plane onto one of several maps. The plane's flight path across the map varies with each round, requiring players to quickly determine the best time to eject and parachute to the ground. Players start with no gear beyond customized clothing selections which do not affect gameplay. Once they land, players can search buildings, ghost towns and other sites to find weapons, vehicles, armor, and other equipment.

Development

On 2 September 2020, the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India banned PUBG Mobile along with 117 other Chinese apps, citing them to be "prejudicial to sovereignty and integrity of India, defence of India, security of state and public order".

In May 2021, Krafton announced the launch of Battlegrounds Mobile India, a successor to the banned PUBG Mobile. Krafton has stated that the game will be available only in India. The company has also made changes to the game to cater to the Indian market, including changing the color of blood to green and adding a feature to limit the amount of time players can spend on the game.

Release

The game was made available for pre-registration on the Google Play Store on 18 May 2021. The game's early access was released for Android users on 17 June 2021. The game was officially released on 2 July 2021 for Android devices. The iOS version was released on 18 August 2021.
